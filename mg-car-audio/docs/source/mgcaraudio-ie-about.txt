Apple CarPlay & Android Auto Installation in Dublin
At MG CAR AUDIO, we specialise in professional Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installations for a wide range of vehicles. Whether your car has a factory screen or requires a complete radio upgrade, we provide reliable solutions that bring modern smartphone connectivity to your vehicle.
Enjoy safe, hands-free access to your favourite apps, including Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, Spotify, WhatsApp, phone calls, and text messages—all displayed directly on your car's screen. Our installations are designed to integrate seamlessly with your vehicle while maintaining an OEM-style appearance and preserving original features wherever possible.
We install both wired and wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto systems, allowing you to connect your smartphone effortlessly every time you drive. Our experienced technicians ensure every installation is completed to the highest standard using quality components for long-lasting performance.
We work with most major vehicle manufacturers, including BMW, Audi, Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Lexus, Ford, Mazda, Hyundai, Kia, Skoda, SEAT, Renault, Peugeot, Citroën, Volvo, Land Rover, and many more. Whether your vehicle is a European, Japanese, or imported model, we can recommend the best solution to suit your needs and budget.
