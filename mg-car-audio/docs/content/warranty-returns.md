## Our fitting warranty

Every installation we carry out at our Dublin 12 workshop comes with a **fitting warranty** on our workmanship. If a connection, wiring or fitting we did develops a fault, bring your car back and we'll put it right free of charge.

### What's covered

- Wiring, connections and mounting carried out by our technicians
- Any fault caused by the way the product was fitted

### What's not covered

- Damage from accidents, water ingress or misuse
- Work done on the system by anyone else after we've fitted it
- Software changes made by a main dealer or manufacturer update, which can sometimes switch off retrofitted features such as CarPlay or video in motion. Bring it back and we'll check it; re-activation may be charged
- Parts you supplied yourself (for example, an Android radio you bought elsewhere). We guarantee our fitting, and the part is covered by its seller's warranty

## Warranty on parts

Parts we supply are covered by the manufacturer's or supplier's warranty, and by your statutory rights. If a part we supplied and fitted becomes faulty, contact us first: we'll diagnose it and handle the warranty claim for you.

## Returning an item bought online

If you buy a product online and we haven't fitted it, you have **14 days from the day you receive it** to tell us you'd like to cancel, without giving a reason. You then have another 14 days to send or bring it back.

- Items must be returned unused, in their original packaging, with all parts and accessories.
- We'll refund you, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving the item back. You pay the cost of returning it unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one.
- We may reduce the refund if the item has been handled more than you'd need to check it, for example if it's been wired in or its seals broken.
- Collected from our workshop? You can bring it back to the workshop instead.

To start a return, email or call us with your order number.

## Services and fitting

- If you cancel a booked fitting, our deposit terms apply: the €50 deposit is non-refundable if you don't attend or cancel with less than 48 hours' notice, and with 48 hours' notice or more we'll move it to a new date.
- Once a fitting has been fully carried out at your request, the 14-day cancellation right no longer applies to that service.

## Faulty items

If something you bought from us is faulty, you're entitled to a repair, a replacement or, in some cases, a refund, under the Consumer Rights Act 2022. Contact us and we'll sort it quickly. Your statutory rights are not affected by anything on this page.
