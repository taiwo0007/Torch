## Delivery in Ireland

We deliver across the Republic of Ireland.

| Option | Cost | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard delivery | €7.95 | 2 to 4 working days |
| Free standard delivery | Orders over €150 | 2 to 4 working days |
| Click and collect, Dublin 12 | Free | Usually ready the next working day |

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are usually dispatched the same day. You'll get an email with tracking as soon as your order is on its way.

## Northern Ireland, the UK and further afield

We don't currently ship outside the Republic of Ireland. If you're outside Ireland and interested in a product, contact us and we'll see what we can do.

## Click and collect

Choose **Pick up** at checkout and collect from our workshop at Unit 3, Ballymount Business Centre, Ballymount Road Lower, Dublin 12, D12 YX27. We'll email you when your order is ready. Please bring your order number.

Collection hours: Monday to Friday 9:30am to 7pm, Saturday 11am to 7pm.

## Fitted products and services

Installation services, such as CarPlay installation, radio fitting and Japan-to-Europe conversions, are carried out at our Dublin 12 workshop, so there's nothing to ship. If you buy a product and want us to fit it, choose click and collect and [book a fitting](/pages/book-a-fitting): we'll keep it ready for your appointment.

## Damaged or missing items

If your order arrives damaged or something is missing, contact us within 48 hours with your order number and a photo, and we'll put it right. See our [Warranty & Returns](/pages/warranty-returns) page for returns.
