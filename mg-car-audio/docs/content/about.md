## Ireland's CarPlay and car audio specialist

At MG Car Audio, we make the car you already love feel brand new inside. From Apple CarPlay and Android Auto on your factory screen to widescreen upgrades and premium sound, we bring the latest in-car technology to your dashboard with a clean, factory-quality finish.

## Our story

We started out doing what we still love most: steering wheel and CarPlay screen installations for BMW and Mercedes-Benz. Today we upgrade Audi, Volkswagen and all major makes too, turning everyday cars into modern, connected ones that are a pleasure to drive.

## Who we are

We're a Dublin car audio and in-car technology team dedicated to one thing: a better drive. Our range includes trusted brands such as JBL and Harman Kardon, so every journey sounds clear, detailed and powerful.

## What we believe

**Every drive should feel like a concert.** Our vision is to make high-quality sound and smart connectivity accessible to everyone, from music lovers to people who just want easier calls and navigation on the school run.

**Quality you can see and hear.** We're committed to excellence in every job: clear product information, honest advice, and installations tailored to your car.

**You come first.** We keep improving what we offer so our products and service don't just meet your expectations, they exceed them.

## Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation in Dublin

We specialise in professional Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installations for a wide range of vehicles. Whether your car has a factory screen or needs a complete radio upgrade, we'll find a reliable way to bring modern smartphone connectivity to your dashboard.

Enjoy safe, hands-free access to Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, Spotify, WhatsApp, calls and messages, all on your car's screen. Our installations are designed to look and work like the factory fitted them, keeping your original features wherever possible.

We install both wired and wireless CarPlay and Android Auto, using quality components for long-lasting performance.

### Why drivers choose MG Car Audio

- Wireless and wired CarPlay and Android Auto
- Apple Maps, Google Maps and Waze navigation
- Spotify, Apple Music and your other streaming apps
- Hands-free calls and messages, with Siri and Google Assistant
- OEM-style integration that keeps your original features wherever possible
- Professional installation, with expert support before and after
